# Publication checklist

Before making the first public commit or enabling external contributions:

- Confirm repository ownership/name, visibility, and who can write or administer it.
- Approve a clear license for original documentation; evaluate future code/tool licensing separately. Do not auto-relicense upstream material.
- Review every included file for game bytes/assets, personal/private records, credentials, unapproved captures and unpublished third-party material.
- Check that citations resolve to pinned public commits. Confirm claims are scoped and do not fabricate runtime validation.
- Preserve upstream authorship and trademark/non-affiliation statements.
- Ensure no BBPC internal private paths, operational authority or source patches are imported by default.
- Demonstrate a fresh-agent cold-start read on a specific question; review its unsupported claims before release.

Public visibility and code-license permission are separate decisions.
