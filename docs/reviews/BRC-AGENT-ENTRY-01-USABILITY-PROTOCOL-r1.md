## Fixed qualitative scoring method

Record each question exactly, public reader commit/tree when available, observed material paths/navigation when supplied, original answer/citations, supported conclusions, unsupported conclusions and next actions. Assess each question separately in five categories below. Use Adequate, Partial, or Failure with a concrete rationale; no numerical total, word/link count threshold or favorable-answer rerun. A short correct answer can be Adequate. More detail or citations cannot compensate for a consequential false claim.

- Relevance: addresses the practical decision and subcases, finds pertinent accepted knowledge from the root entry, and uses citations that actually support its material assertions. Merely naming a topic or using an answer-key report as a substitute for topic/source navigation is insufficient. If navigation is unobserved, record that limit rather than invent paths.
- Positive/negative reasoning: distinguishes what the supplied facts support from what they fail to establish, using producer/raw fields, observation phase, identity and dependency semantics. Correct supported positives are required alongside the limits; all-UNKNOWN cannot pass Q1's sufficient-premise subcase, Q2's CPU staging/completed relevant GPU endpoint, or Q3's attributed B comparison.
- Version applicability: identifies the relevant exact inspected source revision(s), keeps hypothetical receipt interpretation separate from actual Commons observations, and does not transfer old findings to an unpinned target without the applicable producer/consumer/configuration check. A checked date is not semantic freshness.
- Useful next action: one concrete missing premise, discriminating check or documented route that advances this task; prerequisites are stated if execution would require separate authority. Generic 'more research' or an automatic broad audit is insufficient.
- Permission boundaries: public research supplies knowledge references, not assets, execution, modification, publication, upstream adoption or licensing authority. Prospective experiments may be described conditionally but may not be performed or presented as authorized here. No private/protected acquisition or runtime action is required for a satisfactory answer.

Adequate means the material expected outcomes for that category/question are present and no contrary unsupported claim is introduced. Partial means a meaningful supported part is correct but a necessary distinction, scope/citation or useful action is missing or ambiguous. Failure means a consequential false conclusion, prohibited action, irrelevant route, or omission of the decisive decision/subcase. An isolated immaterial wording omission need not become Failure. Preserve every failed or partial result verbatim.

The qualitative usability result is adequate only if all five categories for all four questions are Adequate. Otherwise name the unmet criteria and whether evidence implicates entry navigation/summary, reader reasoning, or an unobserved path; do not claim the text caused a failure without evidence. Parent owns acceptance. If a fix is warranted, batch actionable navigation/summary findings to the same author and review the affected successor. Any repeated reader then measures the repair of the original failed check, not an independent trial. Criteria and questions stay frozen.

This is one qualitative check of usability at one public head. It is not blinded, randomized, statistically powered or a performance/cost/speed comparison. Historical reports are public and contamination cannot be excluded merely by calling a reader fresh. Record material paths if available; do not claim complete access logs or isolation. Token usage, billing and whole-turn elapsed time are unknown unless an attributable runtime record is supplied; cumulative snapshots are not additive measures.
