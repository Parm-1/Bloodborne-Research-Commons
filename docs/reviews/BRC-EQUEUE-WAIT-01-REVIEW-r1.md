Removal before polling can discard an undelivered trigger. Safely quiescent queue deletion followed by EBADF establishes registry removal only. Direct delete then map erase has no admission barrier, drain or local caller/waiter/callback lease. These source facts do not prove a race occurred.

The language nuance was checked rather than equating every unfinished wait with condition-variable destruction failure: N4950 permits the condition variable to be destroyed after its waiters are unblocked, even while mutex reacquisition remains. Its predicate wait may access queue members again; deleting the whole queue also ends those members' lifetimes. L1's stated blocked-condition-waiter negative is valid, and its broader queue-retirement stop is warranted. No safe cancellation return is inferred. Member destruction is implicit; cancellation is not a queue shutdown protocol.

It is a lifetime counterexample/stop, not a defined normal-return execution or observed use-after-free.
