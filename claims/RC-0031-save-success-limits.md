Successful mount/write/close/unmount do not by themselves establish durable complete save replacement. Mount ignores file-mount and write_param results and readonly mode only prints; dirty-copy close ignores host close and parent open/fsync/close errors; unmount changes routes/slot use without committing or waiting for open Files. Metadata write_atomic checks its fwrite/fflush/file-fsync/fclose/rename chain but does not synchronize the directory; write_param discards its status. This supplies no checked persistence endpoint for the named whole save.
