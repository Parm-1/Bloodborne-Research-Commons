Given a stable read-capable descriptor/host description, applicable native ABI, representable nonwrapping destination/size/offset, destination read/write lifetime and writer order through return, and normally returning hooks/touches/host calls, do_read/do_pread notify the requested span then touch each intersected4KiB page before the host attempt. A positive final result n causes post-note of prefix n, increments successful-call/byte diagnostics and returns n. Short positive results are not filled. Zero/error does not publish a positive post-note; pre-note/touch may already have occurred. This establishes the final attempt's reported transfer under supplied host semantics, not original asset correctness, failed-attempt rollback or downstream completion.
