For valid initialized normal paths under successful host synchronization, a sufficient-count wait/poll subtracts its whole need immediately without consulting the linked queue. Otherwise blocking waiters append at the tail, but signal scans past unfittable requests to grant later fitting ones. Queue insertion order therefore does not establish strict FIFO acquisition or host return order; attr0/1/2 does not select a service policy in this implementation.
